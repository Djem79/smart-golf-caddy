Frequently asked questions

The app's interface is in Russian, so screen and button names below are shown in quotes exactly as they appear in the app.

How do I start a round?

On the home screen, tap "Начать новый раунд" ("Start new round"), pick or search for a nearby course, and follow the prompts — a solo round starts right away, a shared round opens a lobby first.

How do I invite a second player?

The lobby screen shows a "Код лобби" ("Lobby code") and a QR code — send the code or show the QR code to the other player, and they enter it or scan it to join.

Why isn't the distance to the green showing?

Two things are required: location permission for the app, and a marked green on that course (your own mark, or an averaged one from other players). If either is missing, the distance isn't shown.

How do I delete my account?

Open the profile screen and tap the "Удалить аккаунт" ("Delete account") button. Details on exactly what gets deleted are in the privacy policy.
