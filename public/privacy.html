Smart Golf Caddy

Privacy Policy

Effective from August 29, 2026.

Who we are

Smart Golf Caddy is an app for tracking golf rounds (web and iOS, with an Apple Watch companion). This page explains what data the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

What data is collected

Account

  • User identifier
  • Name and avatar from your Google account (when signing in with Google Sign-In)
  • Email address

Profile

  • The clubs in your bag
  • Unit of measurement (meters or yards)
  • Handicap

Rounds

  • Score for each hole
  • Which club was used for each shot
  • Shot distance in meters
  • Course name and coordinates
  • Round date and the list of players in a shared round

Location data

The app uses location in three separate, non-overlapping scenarios:

  1. When a user marks a green's center on the map, that point's coordinates are saved on the server and linked to their user ID. This is a static course landmark, not the player's movement path. Marks from different users are averaged together so the course data becomes more accurate for everyone over time.
  2. When searching for nearby courses, the device's current coordinates are sent to the Google Places service to retrieve a list of nearby courses.
  3. Shot distance is calculated on the device itself (from the "before" and "after" coordinates of a shot); only the resulting distance in meters is sent to the server. The player's movement path on the course is never stored or transmitted.

Technical error data

The app uses Sentry to collect crash and error reports. Sending personal data as part of these reports is explicitly disabled in configuration (sendDefaultPii: false).

How data is collected

Data reaches the app through three distinct channels:

  • Entered directly by you — the clubs in your bag, your handicap, your unit of measurement, hole-by-hole scores, and a course name when you add one manually.
  • Received automatically from Google at sign-in — your user identifier, name, avatar, and email address, provided by Google Sign-In when you authenticate.
  • Read through your device's system interfaces — location data, only with your explicit permission, and only while the hole-tracking screen is open or while searching for nearby courses. The app does not access location in the background or outside those moments.

Who data is shared with

  • Google — Firebase (authentication and database), Google Sign-In (sign-in with a Google account), Google Places API (nearby course search).
  • Resend — sending the round-summary email to players in a shared round.
  • Sentry — crash and error reports.

Data is never sold to third parties and never used for advertising.

Data protection at third parties

Data is shared only with the service providers listed above — Google, Resend, and Sentry — and only for the purposes described. Each of them processes data under its own terms of service and is required to maintain a level of protection no lower than what is described in this policy. We do not share data with any other party.

How long data is kept

Account and round data is kept for as long as your account exists. Once you delete your account, it is removed immediately, as described under account deletion below.

Backups of the database are kept longer than the data itself: point-in-time recovery covers the last 7 days, and daily snapshots are retained for 98 days. This means that after you delete your account, your data can still exist inside a backup for up to 98 days, until that backup itself expires and is removed.

Crash and error reports are retained by Sentry under Sentry's own retention schedule; no personal data is included in them.

User rights and account deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app: open the profile screen and tap the "Удалить аккаунт" ("Delete account") button. Deletion does the following:

  • Your profile, statistics, green marks, and solo rounds are permanently deleted.
  • In shared rounds with other players, your name is replaced with "Удалённый игрок" ("Deleted player") — the score and shots themselves remain, so the round history of other participants isn't broken.
  • Your account is fully deleted — signing back in is no longer possible.

For any questions about your data, contact tdm.979@gmail.com.

Withdrawing consent

  • Location — revoke location permission in your operating system's settings (iOS: Settings → the app → Location; in a browser: the site's permission settings). After that, distance to the green and shot-distance measurement stop working; the rest of the app continues to work normally.
  • Everything else — withdraw consent by deleting your account, using the "Удалить аккаунт" ("Delete account") button on the profile screen.
  • Round-summary emails — these are sent to the players in a round you take part in. To stop receiving them, don't join shared rounds, or delete your account. There is currently no separate unsubscribe option.

Security

All traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. Access to your data is restricted by database rules — you can only see your own data and rounds you are a participant in. Score updates go through server-side functions that check permissions before writing, and all calls are protected by App Check.
